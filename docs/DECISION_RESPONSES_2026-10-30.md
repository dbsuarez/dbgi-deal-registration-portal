## What response 1 would change in the portal

If the other responses agree, the build tasks are:

- **Withdrawal (B2, A3):** partner withdrawal with DBGI confirmation; restore with the original place within 30 days. Needed for launch.
- **Status label (A4):** replace "Changes requested" with clearer wording.
- **Project outcome (B4):** partners report Won, Lost, or Cancelled; DBGI confirms.
- **Ties (B5):** a DBGI tie-break decision recorded with its reason.
- **Reposted tenders (B7):** a repost is a new opportunity with its own ranking.
- **Early registrations (B6):** 60-day validity, reminder 7 days before, then DBGI review.
- **Price approvals (C1, C2, C4, C5):** 60-day default validity with a 7-day reminder; prices treated as VAT-inclusive; non-catalog items reviewed only after catalog onboarding; list prices hidden from partners.
- **Repeat orders (D1):** a required re-order marker on the purchase order or project title.
- **Operations (D3, D4):** 5-year retention, daily backups, one-working-day recovery; deadlines counted in calendar days.

No change: B1, C3, D2 (keep today).
