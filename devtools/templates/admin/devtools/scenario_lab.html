What this tool does

Scenario Lab creates repeatable local test data and explains the expected workflow result. It is available only to Django superusers while development mode is enabled.

Users, companies, products, permissions, and login accounts are master data and are always preserved. Optional clearing removes registrations and their items, attachments, events, suggestions, review decisions, opportunities, and opportunity scopes.
