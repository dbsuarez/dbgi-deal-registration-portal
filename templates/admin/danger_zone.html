Snapshots and backups

A snapshot saves all portal data: users and passwords, companies, products, prices, End-users, deals, settings, and uploaded files. Take one before a practice session. Restoring it afterwards puts everything back exactly as it was, and removes what was added since. This is available only in debug mode.

A snapshot stays on this server. Click Download to keep a backup file on your computer. To bring a backup file back, upload it below, then restore it from the list.
